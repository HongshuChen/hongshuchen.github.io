PhD @ University of Edinburgh

Email: hongshu.chen@ed.ac.uk

I am a PhD student at the University of Edinburgh, in the UKRI AI Centre for Doctoral Training in Dependable and Deployable AI for Robotics (CDT-D2AIR). My research focuses on building robots that can adapt to the physical world. I am broadly interested in embodied AI, robot learning, robot foundation models, and robot manipulation.

I received both my B.Eng. and M.Eng. degrees from Northeastern University, China. During my master’s studies, I was a member of the Machine Vision and Robotics (MVR) Lab, supervised by Assoc. Prof. Kechen Song.

📢 If you are interested in working together, please feel free to reach out by email.
